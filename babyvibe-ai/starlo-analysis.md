## KEY PSYCHOLOGY TRIGGERS USED

1. **Sunk Cost Escalation**: 40+ questions before any ask = massive time investment
2. **Barnum/Forer Effect**: Vague but personal-sounding validations ("rare depth", "crystal clear")
3. **Commitment Consistency**: Repeated "yes" answers prime for purchase
4. **Curiosity Gap**: Blurred portrait you can almost see
5. **Scarcity/Urgency**: Countdown timers, "this price expires when you leave"
6. **Social Proof**: 60M+ readings, testimonials, media logos, star ratings
7. **Authority**: "Psychic artists", "cosmic signals", zodiac specificity
8. **Anchoring**: $49.99/mo anchor makes $1 feel like stealing
9. **Gamification**: Spin the wheel "wins" the discount
10. **Personalization Illusion**: "47 signals captured" = feels hyper-specific
11. **Camera Access**: Ultimate commitment — if you gave your FACE, you'll give $1
12. **Sliding Scale Pricing**: Makes $1 feel generous, not cheap
13. **Post-Purchase Upsells**: Priority queue, VIP, chat — squeeze more after the sale
14. **Hope Engineering**: "You're closer than you think" = emotional hook for lonely people

- **31+ micro-commitments** before the paywall
